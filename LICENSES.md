## Reusable components

The generic layouts, shortcodes, projection code, and metadata-adapter
foundations under `copier-template/.orinoco-lite/` were adapted from the
MIT-licensed `leej3/orinoco-lite-demo`. Demo records, evidence, decisions,
branding, site configuration, and the unlicensed presentation snapshot are not
included.

The vendored Congo theme is derived from `leej3/congo` commit
`3623fa505ee42fee899844d94a4ff7f5a1ae9096`. Its MIT license is preserved at
`copier-template/.orinoco-lite/site/themes/congo/LICENSE` and at the equivalent
generated consumer path. Embedded notices for bundled open-source libraries
and free icon assets remain in their distributed files. The Font Awesome Pro
`line.svg` asset and its optional sharing configuration are excluded.
